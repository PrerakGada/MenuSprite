Your first few minutes

Choose your readings and appearance in Monitoring & Sprites. CPU, RAM, power, network, and available fan/temperature readings live in your menu bar. Click CPU, RAM, or Power for a closer look at app usage.

Add Claude and Codex usage readings from Quick start, with day-by-day pace colors and editable labels. Icons and text can use separate colors, and readouts have a little breathing room. Keep-awake rules and Permissions & Access are included. Battery charging controls, closed-lid overrides, and the full visual builder are still to come.

Get MenuSprite

A few things to know.

Can I download MenuSprite yet?+

Yes. Version 0.5.5 Preview 1 is available for Apple Silicon Macs on macOS 26 Tahoe or later. Install with Homebrew or download the DMG. It is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. Intel Macs and older macOS versions aren’t supported by this build.

What’s included in the preview?+

System monitoring, configurable menu-bar readouts, CPU/RAM/Power app-usage panels, Permissions & Access, and ordinary keep-awake rules. Sensor availability varies by Mac; CPU-temperature mapping currently targets the tested M5 family. Per-app Power is derived from CPU energy and does not include GPU, display, or other component power. Battery charging controls, closed-lid overrides, the full visual builder, and marketplace are not in this preview.

How do I update MenuSprite?+

For a Homebrew installation, run brew update, then brew upgrade --cask prerakgada/tap/menusprite. For a manual installation, quit MenuSprite, open the new DMG, and drag MenuSprite onto Applications. Choose Replace when Finder asks, then open the app from Applications and eject the installer disk. Your saved settings stay in place.

Is it free and open source?+

Yes. The public preview is free to download and use. The complete source is available on GitHub under the MIT license.

Can it customize every existing menu bar app?+

Menu bar organization and custom items are both part of the vision. The exact control available over other apps’ items still needs to be validated against macOS capabilities. We’re not promising unrestricted control of every app.

What about system data, logs, and scripts?+

The native preview reads system metrics for the monitoring sprites you enable. Permissions & Access shows MenuSprite’s own access and supported settings actions; opening that page does not request permissions. Custom log sources, scripts, and visual building blocks are future work. This browser concept uses example readings and never accesses your Mac’s system logs.

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. Choose your readings and change their appearance in the native app’s Monitoring & Sprites window. The DMG installer also lets you install without Terminal. The more flexible visual builder shown on this page is still a concept.

Is a Windows version planned?+

No. MenuSprite is specifically for the macOS menu bar. Native Mac integration and a small resource footprint are the focus.
